# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = P × R × T

Where:

- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual interest rate
- **T** = Time period in years

## Example

If:

- Principal (P) = $1,000
- Interest Rate (R) = 5%
- Time (T) = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = 1000 × 0.05 × 2

**Simple Interest = $100**

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Calculate the simple interest using the formula above.

## Features

- Easy to use
- Simple calculation
- Uses the standard simple interest formula
